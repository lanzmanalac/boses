[Boses]On-device captions · nothing leaves this device

Follow a lesson you can hear.

Boses captions your teacher on this device, marks the parts it could not hear clearly, and gives you the lesson on paper at the end.

Read this once. The recogniser here does not report per-word confidence, so text it shows is unverified — it passed an audio-quality check, not a correctness check. Marked gaps mean “this part was not reliably captured”. A clear-looking sentence can still be wrong.

Microphone

Boses will ask for microphone access when you start. Put the device on the desk, closest to the teacher you can manage.

3

Room

For the first few seconds Boses learns how loud this room is, so it knows the difference between a teacher and the noise around them. Hold still and let it listen.

4

Lesson vocabulary

Seeding the recogniser with today's terms is what stops a proper noun being heard as something else. Loaded automatically when a lesson file is present.

    Saved lessons

    Everything stays on this device. There is no account, no cloud, and no second copy — so deleting a lesson is permanent.
